But what do you want to DO today?

softplay takes the friction out of getting out the door. Tell it where you're starting and who's coming, whether that's family or friends, and it generates fun ideas for your crew.

There are two ways to play. Free Play is the fast way: flip through idea cards for any location. Playbook is the full planner: answer a few questions about your crew and your day, get four options, and let the wheel decide.

Found an idea you love but aren't ready to go? Founding Members can save cards to their Playground and play them later.
